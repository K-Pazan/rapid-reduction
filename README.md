These are the files and data necessary to duplicate the statistical analyses for the paper “Spall in good time: reassessing bipolar flaking in Late Pleistocene southern Africa” submitted to the *Journal of Archaeological Method and Theory*. To run the RMarkdown file, download the .Rmd file and follow the directions within. You can also view the RMarkdown file [here](https://k-pazan.github.io/rapid-reduction/).

Data may not be published or used for academic, commercial, or any purposes other than personal curiosity without the permission of K. Pazan and V.C. Schmid.
